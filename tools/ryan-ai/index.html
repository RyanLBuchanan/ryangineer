FACULTY SUPPORT · CANVAS · INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN

CTL Virtual Support Assistant.

A conversational guide for everyday teaching and learning support—Canvas troubleshooting, course design, accessibility, and practical faculty development.

To begin, open the conversation window and allow microphone access if you want to speak. You can ask a question about teaching or Canvas.

Independent proof of concept developed by Ryan Buchanan. Not an official Coastal Alabama Community College service or a live CTL staff member. Responses may be inaccurate. Do not submit student records, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Privacy, scope & limitations

The conversational avatar is provided by D-ID and its providers, who may process audio, prompts, and conversation data under their policies. Guidance is general and may not reflect current Coastal procedures. Confirm institutional policies, course-specific decisions, accessibility accommodations, and account issues through authorized college channels. This prototype is separate from Yotee and NowCast.

What can I help with?

Ask about common CTL and faculty-support tasks:

  • My students cannot see a published Canvas module. What should I check?
  • How do I make a scanned PDF more accessible to students?
  • Help me plan an effective weekly learning module in Canvas.

For account-specific or college-policy questions, contact the appropriate Coastal support office. If the avatar fails to open, permit D-ID scripts and verify that www.ryangineer.com remains on the D-ID agent's allowed-domain list.
